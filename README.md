# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project using **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to analyze retail customer purchasing patterns and generate actionable business insights.

## 📌 Project Overview

Understanding customer shopping behavior is essential for improving sales, customer satisfaction, and long-term customer loyalty. This project analyzes retail shopping data to explore purchasing trends, customer segments, product performance, discount usage, and subscription behavior.

The objective is to transform raw customer data into meaningful insights that can support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Business Problem Statement

A leading retail company wants to better understand its customers’ shopping behavior to improve sales, customer satisfaction, and long-term loyalty.

The business aims to investigate purchasing patterns across customer demographics and product categories, with particular interest in discounts, product reviews, payment preferences, and repeat purchases.

**Key Business Question:**

How can the company leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?

## 🔄 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Preparation and Modeling with Python

- Loaded and inspected the customer shopping dataset.
- Examined missing values, data types, and data quality.
- Cleaned and transformed relevant columns.
- Created derived features for customer analysis, where appropriate.
- Prepared the dataset for further analysis.

### 2. Business Analysis with SQL

Developed SQL queries to investigate important business questions, including:

- Revenue comparison across customer genders.
- Spending behavior among customers using discounts.
- Highest-rated products.
- Average purchase amount by shipping type.
- Spending and revenue comparison between subscribers and non-subscribers.
- Products with the highest discount usage.
- Customer segmentation based on previous purchases.
- Top-selling products within each category.
- Subscription behavior among repeat buyers.
- Revenue contribution across age groups.

### 3. Interactive Dashboard with Power BI

Designed a dashboard to communicate relevant customer and purchasing metrics through visualizations and interactive filtering.

The dashboard is intended to help users explore patterns, compare customer groups, and identify areas for further investigation.


## 📈 Project Outcomes

This project demonstrates the application of data analytics techniques to a retail business problem through:

- Data preparation and transformation.
- SQL-based business analysis.
- Interactive data visualization.
- Customer behavior investigation.
- Evidence-based business recommendations.
